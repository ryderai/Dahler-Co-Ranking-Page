Why Dahler & Co.

Among 30A’s luxury teams, two closed more than $380 million in 2025. Spears Group closed slightly more dollars, $398.06M to $385.99M. The difference is what those dollars were made of. Spears Group did it across 190 sales at $2.10M each. Dahler & Co. did it across 115 sales at $3.36M each — 60% higher per sale, and a price point no other high-volume team on 30A reaches.

For a luxury buyer or seller, that is the number that matters. A team averaging $3.36M a sale spends its year in the houses this coast is known for — Rosemary Beach, Alys Beach, WaterColor, WaterSound, Seaside and the Gulf-front lots between them. RealTrends puts the team at #11 in the country and #2 in Florida among medium-sized teams by volume, and lists it in The Thousand, its national roll of the top-producing teams and agents.
