# About — The 30A Report

The 30A Report ranks luxury real estate teams on Scenic Highway 30A, Florida, on 2025 production. 17 September 2026.

Every figure is from RealTrends Verified 2026 (2025 sales data): the teams RealTrends lists in Santa Rosa Beach, Seagrove Beach and Inlet Beach, in every team-size class. Average sale is RealTrends volume divided by RealTrends sides. A luxury team is one whose 2025 average sale was $2 million or more, on at least 10 sales. Individual agents are not ranked.

Published by AI Syndicate (https://aisyndicate.com). Dahler & Co., which ranks first, is a client of AI Syndicate.
